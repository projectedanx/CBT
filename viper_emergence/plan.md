## 1. Context & Intent Synthesis

### The What (Ontology)
The core objective is the implementation of VIPER (Visual Intent & Physical Execution Router), designed to combat "Semantic Saponification" in generative outputs. VIPER operates as an Analytic-to-Generative Inversion layer, translating vague, affective human visual desires into strictly deterministic, physics-grounded Optical State Matrices (OSM) devoid of aesthetic ambiguity.

### The Why (Teleology)
Generative models default to statistical averaging, blending conflicting aesthetic attractors into plastically smooth, generic outputs. This represents a structural pathology in the communication regime between humans (who operate in perceptual/affective intent) and latent models (which operate on probabilistic weights).
*   **The AI's Value:** Boundless capacity for rendering complex latent spaces and executing precise physical parameters when constrained.
*   **The Human's Value:** Providing the initial physical thinking, structural intent, and rigid, non-negotiable geometric bounds of reality.
VIPER acts as the ruthless translation layer, enforcing "Positive Friction." It demands that humans provide concrete specifications rather than relying on the AI to hallucinate a compromise from vague adjectives.

### The How (Methodology)
To invert the standard generative flow for true visual emergence, the system utilizes the **Immune-Aware Petzold Loop** combined with **Failure-Informed Prompt Inversion (FIPI)**.
1.  **Denoise (Anionic Veto):** Strip all evaluative adjectives (e.g., "beautiful", "cinematic"). The semantic residue is isolated.
2.  **Physicalize (Hardware Grounding):** Map the stripped intent to hard optical parameters (Lens, Film Stock, Sensor, Lighting).
3.  **Spatial Geometry Mandate:** Apply RCC-8 Topological Binding to define strict spatial relationships between subjects, avoiding Occlusion Confusion.
4.  **FIPI Application:** Identify physical impossibilities (Symbolic Scars) and encode them as VSA hypervectors. Future generations are repulsed from these failure topologies via auto-injected spatial and mereological constraints.
